Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026 · Last updated: November 9, 2026

In short

  • No account, no sign-up, and we never ask for your name, email or contacts.
  • Your game settings stay on your device.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may collect device identifiers to serve and measure ads.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

This Privacy Policy explains how VMate Chess – Train & Play (package name com.vconekt.chess, "the App") handles information. The App is developed and published by VConekt LLC ("VConekt", "we", "us", "our"). If you have any questions, see Contact us below.

2. Information we collect

Information you give us

None. The App has no accounts, login, chat or forms. We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, photos, contacts or location.

Information stored on your device

The App saves your preferences locally on your device so they are remembered next time you play:

  • Board and piece theme
  • Sound on/off
  • Move hints, move history and undo settings
  • Board flip setting

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. Games against the AI are calculated entirely on your device.

Information collected by advertising partners

The App is free and supported by ads served by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, AdMob may automatically collect:

  • Your device's Advertising ID (Android advertising identifier)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version)
  • Ad interaction data (such as ads viewed or tapped)
  • Diagnostic information related to ad delivery

This data is collected and processed by Google under its own policies. We do not receive information that identifies you personally. Learn more in How Google uses information from apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. How information is used

  • To remember your game settings (on your device only).
  • To show ads that keep the App free, limit how often you see the same ad, and prevent ad fraud.
  • To check your internet connection and whether a newer version of the App is available on Google Play.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your personal information. The only third party that receives data through the App is Google (AdMob), as described above. We may disclose information if required by law.

6. Your choices

  • Personalized ads: On Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads) to delete or reset your Advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads. You will still see ads, but they will be less tailored.
  • Local data: You can delete all data stored by the App by clearing the App's storage in your device settings or uninstalling the App.
  • Sound and hints: You can change these at any time in the App's Settings screen.

7. Data retention and deletion

Settings stored on your device are kept until you clear the App's data or uninstall it. Because we do not collect or store personal data on our servers, there is no account data to delete. Data collected by Google AdMob is retained according to Google's policies.

8. Security

Data sent between the App and Google's ad services is transmitted over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we choose trusted providers and collect as little as possible.

9. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps.

10. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live (for example, the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its processing. Since we do not hold personal data that identifies you, most requests about ad data are best handled through Google's settings and tools. You can still contact us with any privacy request and we will respond within a reasonable time.
